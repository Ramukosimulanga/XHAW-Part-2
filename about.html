WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN JUST GAMING

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena is a gaming and entertainment destination based in Johannesburg, South Africa.

Established in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, the arena was created to provide an exciting environment where gamers, friends, families, schools, companies and esports competitors can come together.

From competitive esports and premium gaming experiences to birthday parties, school outings and corporate team-building events, Next Level offers experiences designed for different groups and occasions.
